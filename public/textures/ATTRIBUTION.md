# Texture attribution

`earth-night.jpg`, `earth-topology.png` and `night-sky.png` are deterministic procedural project assets generated by `scripts/generate-textures.py`. They use generalized Basemap coast/country geometry and synthetic city-light/star placement; they are not NASA photography and do not claim population accuracy.

The files are included under the repository MIT license. If replaced with third-party Earth or star imagery, record the source, license and required attribution here before publishing.
